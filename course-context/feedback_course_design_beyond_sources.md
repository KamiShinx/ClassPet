Ben, 23 Sep, on the web-app course: "obviously beyond the videos, use your judgment on how things should be done in
2026. mind you, they will do it with gemini."

**Why:** his video lists are often dated (2019-2021 Apps Script tutorials, Materialize CSS) or promotional; a course
built only from them teaches outdated practice.

**How to apply:** treat research as evidence, not the syllabus. Bring current practice (modern JS, async/await,
JSON APIs, secrets server-side, concurrency) and design every workflow around the tool the kids will really use
(Gemini for [[project-make-webapp-course]]; Gemini in Antigravity for [[project-make-minecraft-course]]). Verify
current tool facts (limits, features, age rules) with a quick search rather than assuming.

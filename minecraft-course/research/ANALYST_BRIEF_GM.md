# Extra brief for the game-mechanics batches (read after ANALYST_BRIEF.md)

These batches come from a second list the teacher sent after hearing that worldbuilding + character design alone
won't produce "insane game mechanics". Most are Design Doc videos (two playlists: "RPGs and RPG Mechanics" and
"Game Mechanics"), plus GMTK, Extra Credits, Game Design Library and smaller channels.

What we already have (don't re-derive, build on or argue with it): an earlier game-design batch concluded that
what transfers to this class is a handful of checklists, each worth a 20-30 min worksheet: MDA (what the code
does -> what the player does -> how it feels), a risk/reward dial, a boss-role picker, "does this mob ask the
player something different?", and versatile verbs. Its verdict: game design is a missing piece, but a layer on
top of character design, not a third pillar of the course. **Tell us whether your batch changes that verdict.**

Filter every idea through three questions:
1. **Is it a Minecraft thing?** Minecraft already has verbs (mine, place, craft, fight, explore, farm, trade,
   enchant, brew), status effects, day/night, weather, villages, bosses (Wither, Ender Dragon, Warden), loot, XP.
   A kid's mod adds to or bends that game. An idea about turn-based menus, kart tracks or party AI mostly does not
   apply; say so in a line and move on. An idea that bends an existing Minecraft system (a new status effect, a
   biome with its own weather rule, a mob with a telegraphed attack, a loot choice with a cost) is gold.
2. **Can an 11-13-year-old spec it on one page?** If the idea needs a whole game's systems to make sense, it
   doesn't fit a 20-week, 65-min/week project.
3. **Can Gemini plausibly build it as a NeoForge mod?** Items with abilities, mobs with behaviours/attacks, boss
   phases, blocks that do things, effects, structures, biomes/dimensions, advancements are all buildable.
   Big custom UIs, save-system changes, dialogue trees and AI companions are hard. Flag the hard ones.

In `notes/_batch_<LETTER>.md`, in addition to the usual sections, include:
- **Mechanic cards**: 3-8 of the best mechanic ideas from your batch rewritten as a one-line kid-level design
  prompt for a Minecraft mod (e.g. "A potion that makes you stronger the lower your health is: what stops it
  being the best potion?"), each tagged with the source video and timestamp.
- **Verdict line**: does this batch strengthen, weaken or not affect the "layer, not pillar" verdict above, and why.

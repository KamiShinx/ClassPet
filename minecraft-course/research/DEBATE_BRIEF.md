# Debate brief (5 debaters, 2 rounds)

Working dir: `E:/Websites 2026/Vibecoding projects/minecraft-course-research/`.
Read `CONTEXT.md`, then `SYNTHESIS.md`, then the `notes/_batch_*.md` files your role leans on (open per-video
`notes/<id>.md` when you need the evidence behind a claim). You have a seat with a point of view; argue it hard,
but honestly: cite evidence (`notes/_batch_X.md` or `notes/<id>.md` + timestamp), concede what's true, and never
invent facts about the class, the tools or the videos. If you rely on your own knowledge (e.g. about NeoForge,
Blockbench, child development, classroom practice), label it as such.

The decision the debate serves: **what should Ben's 20-week MAKE course be, and what should its "platform" hold?**
Constraints that are NOT up for debate: 8 kids, 11-13, zero coding, Hebrew speakers, own Windows laptops, 20 weeks,
~65 real min/week + ~30 min homework, Minecraft 26.2 + NeoForge + Java 25, Gemini in Antigravity writes the code,
the platform lives on or beside MAKE's existing Firebase-based system.

## Round 1: write `debate/r1_<your-seat>.md` (max ~1,300 words)
1. **Your course shape**: the 20 weeks in blocks, what each block is for, and where the showcases fall.
2. **One typical 65-minute session** in your design, minute by minute.
3. **What the platform holds** (the "tooltips and a place to express creativity"): the actual objects a kid
   creates and fills in, and how they connect to what gets built in Minecraft.
4. **What a kid has at week 20** (be concrete: how many mobs/items/places/pages of lore, what they can explain).
5. **Your three strongest claims**, each with evidence.
6. **How your design fails**, honestly: the most likely way it goes wrong in a real room.
7. **What you expect the other seats to get wrong.**

## Round 2 (you'll be told when): write `debate/r2_<your-seat>.md` (max ~900 words)
Read all five round-1 files. Then: what you concede (be specific), what you still dispute and why, the one idea
from another seat you'd steal, and your revised recommendation in 10 lines or fewer.

Write only inside `debate/`. Reply to the coordinator with 5 lines: your core position and your biggest worry.

## Things you should know first

1. **There is no released grade 6 science test with items.** The newest grade 5 science MITZAV test on RAMA's list is 2013-14 (here as `sci5-2014-*`; the 2012-13, 2011-12, 2010-11 and 2009-10 tests are here too). For grade 6, RAMA's pages offer only specifications: an internal computerised test (2024-25, page `science-heb-6-2024`), and "Tnufa" tests for 2025-26 and 2026-27 (`tnufa-science-6-2026`, `tnufa-science-5-2027`). Their scoring guides are login-protected and no items are published. The three grade 6 specs were already saved in round 1 under `../science/mitzav/`.
   The closest public item material for grade 6 is therefore the RAMA assessment units and performance tasks for grades 5-6 below (sections B-D), plus the 2014 grade 5 test.
2. **English grade 6 national test:** the first one was held in 2024-25 (Tnufa). Both forms (A and B), the scoring guide and the spec are here (`eng6-2025-*`).
3. Grade 5 English MITZAV tests 2017-2020 are here with scoring guides and specs; 2015, 2016 and 2019 test files were fetched and then dropped for size (listed at the bottom).
4. The files named `.doc` are old binary Word files. I extracted text from five of them with `antiword` to read them (listed in `item-types.md`); no converted copy is saved.
5. **Year tags:** תשפ"ה = 2024-25, תשפ"ו = 2025-26, תשפ"ז = 2026-27. The RAMA "units" and "performance tasks" are undated bank items (most are 2006-2020).
6. Everything in the tables was downloaded by me and checked by file signature (PDF starts `%PDF`, `.doc` starts with the OLE signature, `.docx` is a ZIP). I did **not** open every file to read its content; the "what" column comes from the title on the RAMA page the file is linked from, not from the file text. The kind in brackets (test / scoring / spec / task / key) comes from the link label on that page; where a test has two files labelled "scoring" (e.g. `eng5-2017-scoring.pdf` and `eng5-2017-scoring-2.pdf`), one of the two is a summary sheet (its URL contains "rikuz") and the other the full guide; I did not open them to check which is which.

# T02 summary — DesignCourse "Learn UI/UX in 30 Days"

30 videos: **8 core, 14 useful, 8 skip**. All captions read in full except 5 no-caption videos (judged by
title/pattern: D1TFx9iZB08, EKjCC4PwjFo, OiQ54LUy29E, QKrglAsEgY8, C95S7gW2-_U) and the 5.7h "Interactive
UI/UX Crash Course" (cTUD_vCrY7M), which was sampled and confirmed to be a **word-for-word compilation of
every other video in this group** — skip, pure duplicate.

**Pattern confirmed:** the brief's hunch was right. The **review** videos (critiquing viewer submissions) are
the strongest material — short, example-driven, good-vs-bad, almost no Figma mechanics. The **challenge**
(build) videos teach the same UI fundamentals but bury them in 15-30 minutes of Figma clicking, so most land as
"useful" (needs a guide to extract the concept) rather than "core."

**5 best videos:**
1. **I5Vz2pl8pPI** "Form Design DO's and DON'Ts Review" — the strongest single video: concrete, correct form
   fixes (grouping, consistent field styling, empty-space handling) directly reusable for any Sheets-backed
   signup/settings screen.
2. **uMQnKUIXUSI** "Dashboard UI/UX Design Reviews" — the one rule worth quoting verbatim: light background +
   white cards beats gray cards + drop shadows, plus a 12-13px minimum text size and a WCAG contrast check.
3. **SbVZCiYBVoU** "WHAT IS UI/UX?" — the cleanest UI-vs-UX definition in the whole library, zero Figma,
   5 minutes, ketchup-packet analogy that actually lands.
4. **exQKEte8cmQ** "Who made the BEST Responsive Design?" — best worked example of collapsing a 3-column
   layout into 2 without stranding a card.
5. **IUTmJ_ZYDRg** "FINAL Review" — broad, fast, concrete tips across 8 different UI types in 12 minutes;
   a good closing-lesson candidate.

**Duplicates:** cTUD_vCrY7M duplicates the entire group (skip). Within the group itself, each challenge/review
pair covers the same design (nav, hero, card, responsive, form, animation, embellishments, dark mode, sub-page,
dashboard) twice — not a problem, that's the format, but a guide writer shouldn't assign both halves as "watch
this" without trimming.

**Off-topic (skip):** two portfolio/career videos (FsYi-O9-ZVw, 4C43hMGm-6U — explicitly excluded per brief),
the Midjourney/Photoshop AI-asset-generation video (opMic9UdFo8 — wrong kind of "AI" for this course), the
Figma-prototype animation-authoring video (Xc8zxE9pB4w — doesn't map to CSS/JS), the tool-landscape video
(Gz7IhYEYGEA, mostly), and three pure challenge-announcement stubs with no teaching content (X-_eMAaPv5I,
-QGdFPLaW_k, d2ECtM9Ahq4).

**Surprising:** the light/dark-mode video (wqTZuEQ2P-s) has a genuinely reusable rule (shift lightness on the
same hue, don't just invert to black/white) that's exactly the kind of feature request kids will hit building
apps — worth extracting even though the delivery is all Figma hex-picking.

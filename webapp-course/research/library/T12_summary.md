# T12 summary

Group T12 = two playlists: **SAP ABAP debugging** (Rahul Mehta, 27 videos) and **PMP scope/project
management** (PMC Lounge, 34 videos). 6 videos sampled, full captions read (all under 13 min).

**Counts:** skip = 5, useful = 1, core = 0.

**Top pick:** only one video earns a place, and even that needs work:
- `5nRuHDvPDb8` "Scope Management - The Basics" (**useful**) - the one video worth mining. Under the
  PMBOK framing it makes two genuinely transferable points: **product scope vs. project scope**
  (what the app should do/look like, vs. all the work needed to build it) and **scope creep**
  (uncontrolled extra work sneaking in mid-project) - both real hazards for teens scoping a web app.
  Needs a guide to strip the PMP-exam wrapper and re-example it with an app instead of a building.

No other video in the sample clears the bar. The 4 SAP debugging videos (intro, internal-table
edits/Goto Statement, static/dynamic breakpoints, pop-up screen debugging) are all clean, simple
narration - but 100% SAP GUI mechanics (transaction codes, ABAP executable statements, multi-user
shared-server breakpoints, drag-a-notepad-onto-a-popup tricks). None of it maps onto a browser or
Apps Script debugger; "watch a variable while paused" is the only universal idea present and it's
never separated from the SAP clicking. The PMP process-overview video (`LluIn-9oCM4`) is pure
PMBOK jargon (six named "knowledge area processes") with no concrete example - skip.

**Duplicates:** `LluIn-9oCM4` is essentially a table-of-contents recap of content already covered
(and covered better, with examples) in `5nRuHDvPDb8` - don't fetch both if only one slot exists.

**Surprising:** the PMP playlist's early "basics" video is the most useful item in the whole group,
while the SAP playlist is uniformly unusable despite being tonally easy to follow - the problem is
100% topical fit (enterprise multi-user tooling), not difficulty.

# T14 triage summary (teacher-picked singles)

**Counts:** core 5 · useful 4 · skip 3 (11 total, 3 nocaps judged by title only)

## The 5 best
1. **hJHvdBlSxug – How The Web Works: The Big Picture** (Academind, 12 min, full read). The cleanest domain-to-DNS-to-HTTP-to-JSON explanation in the whole group, no padding. This is the "how the web works" video for the course.
2. **WXsD0ZgxjRw – APIs for Beginners: Full Course** (freeCodeCamp/Craig Dennis, 3h07m, sampled). First 18 minutes is the best explanation of "what is an API" anywhere here (remote controls, buttons, streaming apps as analogies); the rest is a well-taught but Twilio/Vue/Node build-along that needs a guide.
3. **wIuVvCuiJhU – world's shortest UI/UX design course** (Juxtopposed, 7 min, full read). One real project, start to finish: user flow, wireframe, design system, six design principles. Names Figma in passing but teaches the transferable process.
4. **YlN28RNChl0 – Junior to Senior UX/UI Designer** (uxpeak, 17 min, full read). Repeated before/after redesigns (sleep app, banking transfer) make hierarchy, recognition-over-recall and progressive disclosure concrete. Two short paid-playbook ad breaks, easy to skip past.
5. **HE4rLEQpiXY – How to think like a GENIUS UI/UX designer** (Kole Jain, 5.5 min, full read). Dense, correct, intent-first design walked through an Airbnb-style example; one short sponsor read.

## Skips and why
- **c9Wg6Cb_YlU** (Figma Tutorial, 86 min, nocaps/title only): straight Figma software tutorial. Kids won't touch Figma — skip per house rule, and wIuVvCuiJhU already gives the transferable process in 7 minutes.
- **zN8YNNHcaZc** (How does the internet work?, 103 min, sampled): turned out to be a **network-hardware** course — switches, routers, ISPs, submarine cables, VPN tunneling, peering — not web/app development. Heavily repetitive. hJHvdBlSxug covers what's actually needed in a tenth of the time.
- **cpkd4EYYQ8o** (How the Web Works, 41 sec): a stray clip, one breath of content, fully redundant with hJHvdBlSxug.

## Duplicates / overlap noticed
- **cpkd4EYYQ8o** duplicates **hJHvdBlSxug** almost exactly, just 18x shorter and far thinner.
- **zjfViRCjT5U** ("How a web application works", nocaps, 35 min) is probably another web-works overlap with hJHvdBlSxug — flagged `useful` rather than `core` until someone confirms it adds something new.
- **EcbgbKtOELY** (Kole Jain, nocaps) likely overlaps conceptually with HE4rLEQpiXY from the same channel.

## Surprising
**oYxTTirKY8M** ("System Design Explained") markets itself as APIs/databases/caching/CDNs but is really an interview-prep course for senior engineers — load balancers, message queues, gRPC, OAuth2, CSRF/XSS defense are all well past a 14-15 y/o building an Apps Script game. But its early SQL-vs-NoSQL segment (6:01–12:37) and HTTP status code segment (1:12:42–1:15:27) are genuinely clean and relevant to how the course frames Sheets-as-database — worth clipping for the guide rather than assigning whole.

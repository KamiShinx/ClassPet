yt-dlp can't fetch (says "not available"). They play in a browser; captions were captured in the browser pane
(`grab_mfk_caps.js`). Human-made English captions, plus a **Hebrew track** ("iw") on every video.

  IP Addresses & DNS.

  How Search Works (index built in advance), Encryption (optional curiosity).

**Pattern confirmed, exactly as predicted.** All three DesignCourse *review* episodes (cards `D1TFx9iZB08`, layout `OiQ54LUy29E`, sub-page `QKrglAsEgY8`) are core: Gary Simon moves fast through real submissions fixing white space, contrast, shadow logic, alignment and decluttering -- concrete, teen-followable, no wasted setup. The two *challenge* episodes (forms `EKjCC4PwjFo`, dashboards `C95S7gW2-_U`) are useful-only: each has 1-3 minutes of genuine concept (minimum viable form fields, input states, dashboard requirements) buried in 15-20 minutes of Figma auto-layout/component/plugin mechanics that don't transfer since Gemini builds the kids' UI, not Figma.

**5 best videos in the group:**
1. `EcbgbKtOELY` (Kole Jain) -- the best single video here: a dense, tool-free 10-minute tour of signifiers, hierarchy, white space, type, color, UI states and microinteractions, all with concrete before/afters. Core, no caveats.
2. `QKrglAsEgY8` -- beyond the usual polish fixes, it has two genuinely transferable UX lessons: a submission gets rejected for scope creep (added labels nobody asked for), and a false-affordance mistake (text styled to look clickable) that echoes Kole Jain's "signifiers" -- nice cross-link.
3. `D1TFx9iZB08` -- tight, fast, the clearest single rule of the batch ("shadows must be darker than their background, never lighter").
4. `OiQ54LUy29E` -- best closing line of the group: "simplify, mind your columns and rows, align with white space" -- a one-sentence design mantra worth quoting on its own page.
5. `zjfViRCjT5U` -- only useful, but its MVC-as-three-teams explanation (model=database, view=what you see, controller=between) is the one thing this group adds that the library's existing Academind/code.org web-basics videos don't cover, and it maps directly onto Sheet+Apps Script+HTML.

**Duplicates / overlap noticed:** `zjfViRCjT5U`'s first ~16 minutes (HTTP request/response, DNS, IP addresses) duplicate ground the library's Academind (12 min) and code.org HTTP&HTML (7 min) videos already cover better and faster -- skip those minutes, keep only the front-end/back-end and MVC clips. QKrglAsEgY8's affordance moment duplicates Kole Jain's "signifiers" concept -- fine as reinforcement, not a reason to cut either.

**Surprising:** `tpTQurUhX_M` (PMP "Create WBS Process") was expected to be a clean skip -- it's 90% exam-prep jargon (knowledge areas, ITTO, scope-baseline equations) totally wrong register for a 14-year-old. But its one worked example, breaking a website project into deliverables (UX/servers/database -> pages/CSS/images, schema/tables), is a genuinely reusable planning pattern, so it scraped into "useful" for that 2-minute clip alone rather than a flat skip.
